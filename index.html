Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

No jargon. No runaround. Just straight answers.

Does this cost my business anything? ▼
Your site assessment and revenue projection are always free. Project costs depend on your site, the number of stalls, and the electrical work required, so before you commit we give you a complete picture: estimated costs, every incentive you qualify for (such as New York's tax credit of up to $5,000 per charger), and projected charging revenue. If paying upfront isn't the right fit, we'll also walk you through financing, smaller starting projects, and partner-funded options where your site qualifies.
How long does the process take? ▼
Timelines depend mostly on utility work and permitting. Con Edison notes its utility process alone can take 8 to 15 months, which is why we start early and run applications, permits, and Tesla coordination in parallel. We'll give you a site-specific timeline after your assessment.
Do I need to own the property? ▼
Not necessarily. We work with property owners, tenants with long-term leases, and property managers. We'll assess your specific situation during the free consultation.
What happens after the chargers are installed? ▼
Your property earns passive revenue from every charging session - 24/7. You set the charging price, and Tesla handles billing, payment processing, network operations, and driver support for a flat $0.10/kWh fee. You collect the revenue. We monitor optimization opportunities on your behalf.
What areas do you serve? ▼
We currently serve all five boroughs of New York City and surrounding areas. We are actively expanding our service territory across the tri-state region.
Are the incentive amounts guaranteed? ▼
Incentive amounts are based on current program guidelines and are subject to eligibility confirmation. Our team works to maximize every available dollar, but actual amounts depend on your specific property and program availability at time of application.
